Coach Outreach Board

How to use the board, what to say, and how to log your contacts

🎯 Getting Started

1
Select your name

Top right corner — tap the dropdown and select your coach profile. Your assigned members will highlight.

2
Filter to your members

Use the Mine filter to see only members assigned to you. Green ✓ tiles have already been contacted this week.

3
Open a member tile

Tap any tile for their attendance data, a suggested conversation opener, and last week's contact notes (if any).

4
Have the conversation, then log it

After speaking with the member, tap Log contact this week — choose method, outcome, and write a short note. This syncs to the shared sheet so everyone has context next week.

💬 What to Say

🟡 Monitor members
  • "Hey [name], how's everything going? We want to make sure training is working for you."
  • "Hey [name], noticed you've been in more consistently — what's been helping?"
  • "Hey [name], noticed it's been a quieter couple of weeks. Everything ok?"
🟢 Maintain members
  • Streak milestone: Call it out in class — "Big shout out to [name] — X weeks straight. That's what consistency looks like."
  • Training interest: "Have you been enjoying what we've been doing? Anything you want more of?"
  • Goals: "You've been so consistent — any goals you want to work toward over the next few months?"
  • Referral: "You're one of our best — do you know anyone who'd love this?"
Don't read the script word for word. Use the data to inform what you say — the best conversations feel natural.

Method
  • Floor
  • SMS

Outcome
  • Positive
  • Needs follow-up

Notes — write one or two sentences
  • "Mentioned she's been busy with work but wants to get back to 4x a week. Tuesday evenings work best."
  • "Really positive chat — excited about the strength cycle. No concerns."
  • "Hasn't been in due to a knee issue. Seeing physio this week. Check in next Monday."

🔑 Your PIN

Each coach has a unique PIN used to reset or remove a contact log entry. It acts as a simple confirmation that the change was intentional — and makes it easy to see who made an edit.

Your PIN has been shared with you privately by Fraser. Keep it to yourself — don't share it with other coaches.

When you need your PIN: tap ↺ Reset all logs on a member's panel, or ✕ to remove a single log entry. You'll be prompted to enter it before anything is deleted.

📅 Weekly Rhythm

Monday

Dashboard refreshes. Fraser reviews urgent contacts and assigns Monitor/Maintain members to coaches. Check the board before your first class.

Tuesday – Saturday

Work through your assigned members across the week. Floor conversations during class are the most natural — no need to seek people out separately.

Sunday

Contact ticks reset automatically. Your notes carry forward as context for next week's conversations.

Quality over quantity. One genuine conversation beats three quick check-ins. The goal is for members to feel known, not processed.
