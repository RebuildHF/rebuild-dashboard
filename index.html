How to read the board and understand the quadrant system

📐 The Four Quadrants

Every member sits in one quadrant based on their attendance over the last 30 days. Quadrant placement updates automatically each week when the dashboard is refreshed.

🔴 DO FIRST — Contact today

Members who have gone missing after being active, flagged At Risk by Wodify, or shown two consecutive weeks of zero attendance. Fraser handles these personally — call or SMS same day.

🟠 SCHEDULE — Re-engage this week

Members averaging fewer than 8 sessions in the last 30 days, or showing a declining pattern over 2–3 consecutive weeks. Fraser schedules a direct conversation.

🟡 MONITOR CLOSELY — Early warning

Members averaging 8–11 sessions in 30 days. Not in crisis but not thriving. A warm floor conversation from their coach can stop a slide before it becomes a problem. These are assigned to coaches via the Coach Outreach Board.

🟢 MAINTAIN — Keep the fire burning

Members averaging 12+ sessions in 30 days. Consistent and engaged. Coaches celebrate milestones, check in on goals, and look for referral opportunities.

📊 How to Read the Data

Weekly windows (Mon–Sun)
  • Last week (v7) — sessions Mon–Sun of the most recently completed week
  • Week before (v714) — the week prior to that
  • 30d — total sessions in the last 30 days (the primary threshold)
  • WoW — week-on-week change (e.g. +2 or −3)
  • Str — consecutive weekly attendance streak
WoW elevation rules
  • Gone last week after 2+ visits the week before → Do First
  • Two consecutive weekly drops while still attending → Schedule
  • Maintain member with a sharp drop last week → Monitor
Filters and views
  • Location tabs — filter the whole board to Wynnum or Capalaba
  • Cards / List toggle — switch each quadrant between chip view and a sortable table
  • ↓ Export button — download any quadrant as an Excel file with outreach scripts
  • Needs action filter — hide already-contacted members to focus on outstanding outreach
  • Mark as contacted — tick a member after speaking with them; syncs across devices

📅 Weekly Update Schedule

  • Sunday evening — dashboard updated with the previous Mon–Sun week's data
  • Monday morning — review Do First and Schedule; assign Monitor/Maintain to coaches
  • Monday reset — contacted ticks clear automatically at the start of each new week
